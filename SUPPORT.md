# Support

Yaya’s Calendar is a free app looked after on a best-effort basis. There is no support contract and no
promised response time, but issues get read.

- **First stop:** [docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md) (Gatekeeper, grey or blank widgets,
  sign-in errors, the Keychain, removing the app) and [docs/GOOGLE-SETUP.md](docs/GOOGLE-SETUP.md) (your
  own Google sign-in, step by step, with the errors a fresh project produces).
- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue at
  <https://github.com/YahyaElghobashy/yayas-calendar-mac/issues/new/choose> with the version from
  **Settings → Version**, your macOS version, clear steps, and the redacted `--diagnose` line. Never attach
  files from the app’s data folder: they hold your mail and meetings.
- **Security:** privately, never in an issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Docs:** [README](README.md), [Privacy](PRIVACY.md), [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md),
  [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

Google account, Gmail or Google Cloud problems that happen outside the app (a locked account, a billing
question, a Workspace policy) are Google’s to answer. Yaya’s Calendar is not affiliated with Google.
